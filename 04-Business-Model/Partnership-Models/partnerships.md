# Partnership Models

## Strategic Partnership Opportunities

### 1. Payroll Providers
- **Partners:** Gusto, ADP, Paychex, Deel
- **Model:** Bidirectional integration; refer customers to each other
- **Value:** We handle HR, they handle payroll — complete solution together

### 2. Accounting Firms
- **Partners:** Big 4 (for enterprise), regional firms (for SMBs)
- **Model:** Referral fees (10–20% of first year) or white-label
- **Value:** Accountants are trusted advisors to SMBs and often asked about HR

### 3. Law Firms (Employment Law)
- **Partners:** Boutique employment law firms
- **Model:** Referral partnership; we handle routine, they handle complex
- **Value:** We reduce their low-value work; they handle escalations

### 4. HR Consulting Firms
- **Partners:** Small HR consultancies
- **Model:** White-label our platform; they add strategic advisory
- **Value:** They expand offerings without hiring; we get distribution

### 5. Business Formation / Corporate Services
- **Partners:** Stripe Atlas, Clerky, Firstbase, local equivalents
- **Model:** Bundle offer — "Start your company, HR included"
- **Value:** Capture companies at formation, when they have zero HR

### 6. Venture Capital / Accelerators
- **Partners:** Y Combinator, Techstars, 500 Global, local VCs
- **Model:** Portfolio company benefit / preferred pricing
- **Value:** Access to hundreds of growing startups simultaneously

### 7. Insurance Brokers
- **Partners:** Group health, workers' comp brokers
- **Model:** Referral partnership
- **Value:** Natural extension of HR — we help employees understand benefits

## Revenue Share Models
| Partnership Type | Our Take | Partner Take |
|-----------------|----------|-------------|
| Referral (to us) | 80–90% | 10–20% of Y1 revenue |
| Referral (from us) | 10–20% commission | 80–90% |
| White-label | 70% | 30% markup |
| Co-sell | Split based on contribution | Negotiated |
