# Revenue Streams

## Primary Revenue: SaaS Subscription (PEPM)
- Per-employee-per-month recurring revenue
- Target gross margin: 80–85% (software + AI inference costs)
- Expected: 85–90% of total revenue

## Secondary Revenue Streams

### 1. Implementation & Setup Fees
- One-time fee for onboarding new customers
- Includes: policy migration, data import, custom configuration
- Range: $500 (Starter) to $10,000 (Enterprise)
- Expected: 3–5% of total revenue

### 2. Professional Services
- Custom policy writing and compliance consulting
- Organizational design advisory
- Compensation benchmarking studies
- HR strategy consulting (delivered by humans + AI)
- Range: $200–$500/hour or project-based
- Expected: 5–8% of total revenue

### 3. Add-On Agents (À La Carte)
- Customers on lower tiers can add individual agents
- Example: Starter customer adds Recruitment Agent for +$10 PEPM
- Expected: Built into PEPM pricing growth

### 4. Marketplace / Partner Revenue (Phase 3+)
- Third-party integrations and plugins
- Revenue share with training providers, benefits brokers, background check services
- Expected: 2–5% of total revenue (future)

### 5. Data & Insights Products (Phase 3+)
- Anonymized, aggregated industry benchmarks
- "State of HR" reports
- Compensation data products
- Expected: Small but high-margin

## Revenue Model Summary

| Year | ARR Target | Primary Driver |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Year 1 | $500K | First 20 customers, prove model |
| Year 2 | $3M | Scale to 100 customers, expand tiers |
| Year 3 | $20M | Enterprise deals, multi-country |
| Year 4 | $60M | Market expansion, full agent suite |
| Year 5 | $150M | Category leadership |

## Key Metrics to Track
- **ARR** (Annual Recurring Revenue)
- **MRR** (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- **ARPU** (Average Revenue Per User/Company)
- **Net Revenue Retention** (target: >120%)
- **Gross Margin** (target: >80%)
- **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- **LTV** (Customer Lifetime Value)
- **LTV:CAC Ratio** (target: >3:1)
