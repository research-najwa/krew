# Burn Rate & Runway Analysis

## Monthly Burn Rate by Stage

| Stage | Monthly Burn | Team Size | Key Costs |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| Pre-seed (Months 1–6) | $15K–$30K | 2–3 (founders) | Infra, LLM APIs, legal |
| Seed (Months 7–12) | $50K–$80K | 5–8 | Team, infra, marketing |
| Post-seed (Year 2) | $100K–$200K | 10–20 | Scaling team, sales, marketing |
| Series A (Year 3) | $300K–$500K | 25–40 | Growth, enterprise sales, expansion |

## Runway Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Bootstrap / Angel ($200K raised)
| Month | Burn | Revenue | Net Burn | Cash Left |
|-------|------|---------|----------|-----------|
| 1–3 | $20K/mo | $0 | $60K | $140K |
| 4–6 | $25K/mo | $5K/mo | $60K | $80K |
| 7–9 | $30K/mo | $15K/mo | $45K | $35K |
| **Runway:** ~9 months | | | | |

### Scenario 2: Pre-Seed ($750K raised)
| Month | Burn | Revenue | Net Burn | Cash Left |
|-------|------|---------|----------|-----------|
| 1–6 | $25K/mo | $0–$5K | $135K | $615K |
| 7–12 | $60K/mo | $15K–$50K | $170K | $445K |
| 13–18 | $80K/mo | $50K–$100K | $80K | $365K |
| **Runway:** ~18–20 months | | | | |

### Scenario 3: Seed ($2M raised)
| Month | Burn | Revenue | Net Burn | Cash Left |
|-------|------|---------|----------|-----------|
| 1–6 | $30K/mo | $0–$5K | $165K | $1.84M |
| 7–12 | $80K/mo | $15K–$50K | $290K | $1.55M |
| 13–18 | $120K/mo | $80K–$150K | $180K | $1.37M |
| 19–24 | $150K/mo | $150K–$250K | $0–$100K | $1.07M+ |
| **Runway:** 24+ months | | | | |

## Rule of Thumb
- Always maintain 6+ months of runway
- Start fundraising when you have 9 months of runway left
- Target 18–24 months of runway per raise
