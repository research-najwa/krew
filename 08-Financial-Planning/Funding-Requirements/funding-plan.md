# Funding Requirements & Strategy

## Funding Roadmap

| Round | Amount | Timing | Milestone to Raise | Use of Funds |
|-------|--------|--------|--------------------|-----------|
| **Pre-Seed** | $500K–$1M | Month 0–3 | Team + idea + early prototype | Build MVP, hire first engineers |
| **Seed** | $2M–$4M | Month 12–15 | 20+ customers, $500K ARR | Scale team, expand agents, marketing |
| **Series A** | $10M–$20M | Month 24–30 | 200+ customers, $5M+ ARR | Go multi-market, enterprise sales |
| **Series B** | $30M–$50M | Month 36–42 | 1000+ customers, $20M+ ARR | International expansion, full platform |

## Pre-Seed Raise Details

### Target: $750K
### Instrument: SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)
### Valuation cap: $5M–$8M

### Use of Funds
| Category | Amount | % |
|----------|--------|---|
| Engineering (2 hires) | $300K | 40% |
| LLM & Infrastructure | $100K | 13% |
| Legal & Compliance | $50K | 7% |
| Marketing & Sales | $100K | 13% |
| Founder salaries (12 months) | $150K | 20% |
| Buffer | $50K | 7% |

### Target Investors
- [ ] Angel investors with HR or SaaS background
- [ ] Pre-seed funds (Precursor, Hustle Fund, etc.)
- [ ] Accelerators (Y Combinator, Techstars, Antler)
- [ ] Regional investors (STV, Raed Ventures, 500 Global MENA)
- [ ] Strategic angels (HR executives, AI leaders)

## Fundraising Preparation Checklist
- [ ] Financial model (spreadsheet)
- [ ] Pitch deck (10–12 slides)
- [ ] Executive summary (1 page)
- [ ] Product demo (even if prototype)
- [ ] Cap table setup
- [ ] Data room (legal docs, financials, team bios)
- [ ] Target investor list with warm intros
- [ ] Practice pitch (10+ dry runs)
