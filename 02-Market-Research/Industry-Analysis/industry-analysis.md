# HR Industry Analysis

## Industry Overview
The global Human Resource Management (HRM) market encompasses all software, services, and outsourcing related to managing people within organizations.

## Key Market Segments

### 1. HR Software (HCM / HRIS)
- **What it is:** Software platforms for managing HR processes
- **Key players:** Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle HCM, BambooHR, Gusto, Rippling
- **Market size:** ~$25B+ (2024), growing 10–12% CAGR
- [ ] Research latest market size figures

### 2. HR Outsourcing (HRO)
- **What it is:** Companies outsource HR functions to third parties
- **Key players:** ADP, Paychex, TriNet, Insperity
- **Market size:** ~$35B+ globally
- **Relevance:** Our direct competitor category — we're AI-native HRO

### 3. Professional Employer Organizations (PEOs)
- **What it is:** Co-employment model where PEO becomes employer of record
- **Key players:** TriNet, Justworks, Deel, Papaya Global, Oyster
- **Market size:** ~$60B+ (US alone)
- **Relevance:** Similar value prop (outsourced HR) but we don't co-employ

### 4. Employer of Record (EOR)
- **What it is:** Legally employs workers on behalf of companies in foreign countries
- **Key players:** Deel, Remote.com, Oyster, Papaya Global, Velocity Global
- **Market size:** ~$5B+ and growing rapidly
- **Relevance:** Potential partnership or expansion path

### 5. HR Consulting
- **What it is:** Advisory services for HR strategy, compliance, org design
- **Key players:** Mercer, Korn Ferry, Deloitte HC, McKinsey People & Org
- **Market size:** ~$30B+
- **Relevance:** Our agents replace day-to-day consulting
