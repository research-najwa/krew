# Revenue Projections (5-Year Model)

## Assumptions
- Average PEPM: $25 (blended across tiers)
- Average employees per customer: 100 (growing over time)
- Monthly churn rate: 2% (improving to 1% by Year 3)
- Net revenue retention: 115% (customers grow headcount + upgrade)
- Sales cycle: 3–4 weeks average
