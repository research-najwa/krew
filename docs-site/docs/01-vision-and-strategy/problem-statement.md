# Problem Statement

## The Problem

### For Small & Mid-Size Companies (10–500 employees)
- **Can't afford a full HR team** — A competent CHRO costs $150K–$300K+/year. An HR team of 3–5 people costs $400K–$800K/year.
- **Rely on ad-hoc HR** — Founders, office managers, or finance leads "do HR on the side," leading to compliance gaps, poor hiring, and employee dissatisfaction.
- **Use fragmented tools** — Cobble together 5–10 SaaS tools (ATS, payroll, benefits, LMS) with no integration or strategic layer on top.
- **Reactive, not proactive** — Only deal with HR when something goes wrong (lawsuits, turnover spikes, failed audits).

### For Large Companies (500+ employees)
- **HR is expensive and slow** — Typical HR-to-employee ratio is 1:100. A 2,000-person company has 20 HR staff.
- **Inconsistent quality** — HR depends on individual competence. Some HR business partners are excellent; others are not.
- **Buried in administrative work** — 60–70% of HR time goes to transactional tasks (paperwork, queries, compliance tracking) rather than strategic work.
- **Compliance risk** — Especially for multi-country operations, keeping up with labor laws is a full-time job.

### For Employees (the end user)
- **Poor HR experience** — Slow responses, unclear policies, inconsistent treatment.
- **No 24/7 support** — HR operates on business hours; employee needs don't.
- **Lack of transparency** — Employees don't know their rights, benefits, or growth paths because HR doesn't communicate proactively.

## The Root Cause
HR is a **knowledge-intensive, process-heavy, regulation-bound** function that has been underinvested in technology. Current HR tech automates *workflows* (e.g., applicant tracking, payroll processing) but doesn't automate *judgment, strategy, or expertise* — which is where AI agents come in.

## The Opportunity
AI agents can now:
- Understand and apply complex labor regulations
- Make consistent, bias-reduced decisions
- Operate 24/7 across time zones and languages
- Learn and improve from every interaction
- Scale from 10 to 10,000 employees with near-zero marginal cost

**This is the first time in history that the full HR function can be delivered as a service through software.**
