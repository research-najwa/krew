# Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

## Primary ICP: Growth-Stage Companies (50–500 employees)

### Profile
- **Company size:** 50–500 employees
- **Stage:** Series A–C, scaling rapidly
- **Current HR setup:** 1–3 HR generalists, stretched thin
- **Pain:** Hiring fast, staying compliant, building culture — with too few HR people
- **Budget:** Can afford $5K–$20K/month for HR but can't afford $500K+ for a full HR team
- **Decision maker:** CEO, COO, or VP of People
- **Why they buy:** They need enterprise-grade HR NOW but can't build it fast enough

### Characteristics
- Hiring 5–20 people per month
- Operating in 1–3 countries
- Using 3–7 disconnected HR tools
- Have had at least one compliance scare or employee issue
- Tech-savvy, open to AI solutions

---

## Secondary ICP: Small Businesses (10–50 employees)

### Profile
- **Company size:** 10–50 employees
- **Current HR setup:** None — founder or office manager handles HR
- **Pain:** No HR expertise, compliance risk, poor onboarding, reactive to issues
- **Budget:** $500–$3,000/month
- **Decision maker:** Founder / CEO
- **Why they buy:** They know they need HR but can't justify a full-time hire

---

## Tertiary ICP: Enterprises (500–5,000 employees) — Phase 2

### Profile
- **Company size:** 500–5,000 employees
- **Current HR setup:** Full HR department (10–50 people)
- **Pain:** HR is expensive, slow, and inconsistent. Too much admin, not enough strategy.
- **Budget:** $50K–$200K/month (still cheaper than their current HR cost)
- **Decision maker:** CHRO, CFO
- **Why they buy:** Augment their HR team — let AI handle 70% of transactional work

---

## Anti-Personas (Who We Do NOT Sell To Initially)
| Segment | Why Not |
|---------|---------|
| Solopreneurs / <10 employees | Too small, low revenue potential |
| Highly regulated industries (healthcare, finance) | Complex compliance needs — address later |
| Government entities | Long sales cycles, procurement complexity |
| Companies with strong unions | Union dynamics add complexity to AI HR |

## Customer Discovery Questions
Use these in interviews with potential customers:

1. How do you handle HR today? Who is responsible?
2. What are your biggest HR headaches right now?
3. How much do you spend on HR (people + tools + outsourcing)?
4. Have you ever had a compliance issue? What happened?
5. How do employees get answers to HR questions today?
6. Would you trust AI to handle [onboarding / payroll / compliance]?
7. What would you pay for a complete AI HR solution per employee per month?
8. What's your biggest fear about using AI for HR?
