# Pitch Deck Outline (10–12 Slides)

## Slide 1: Title
- Company name + logo
- One-line tagline: "Your AI HR Department"
- Founder name(s) and title(s)
- Contact information

## Slide 2: Problem
- Companies can't afford great HR
- SMBs: No HR at all, or 1 overwhelmed generalist
- Growing companies: Fragmented tools, compliance risk, poor employee experience
- The cost of bad HR: lawsuits, turnover, lost productivity
- **Key stat:** [Insert: avg cost of HR department, compliance fine, etc.]

## Slide 3: Solution
- AI agents that replace the entire HR department
- 10 specialized agents covering every HR function
- Always on, always compliant, always consistent
- Visual: Show the agent ecosystem diagram

## Slide 4: Product Demo / Screenshots
- Show the platform in action
- Employee asking a question via Slack → agent responding
- Admin dashboard with compliance status
- Onboarding workflow in progress

## Slide 5: How It Works
- Company signs up → uploads policies (or AI generates them)
- Agents deploy across Slack, email, web portal
- Employees interact naturally → agents handle requests
- Admins get analytics dashboard
- Human escalation for sensitive situations

## Slide 6: Market Opportunity
- TAM: $XX billion (HR software + HR outsourcing + PEO)
- SAM: $XX billion (target segments and geographies)
- SOM: $XX million (achievable in 3 years)
- Key trend: AI replacing knowledge work functions

## Slide 7: Business Model
- Per-employee-per-month subscription ($15–$50 PEPM)
- 3 tiers: Starter, Growth, Enterprise
- 85%+ gross margins
- Strong unit economics: LTV:CAC >10x
- Net revenue retention >115%

## Slide 8: Traction (When Available)
- Waitlist signups
- Beta customers and logos
- Revenue / ARR
- Key metrics (resolution rate, NPS, retention)
- Customer quotes / testimonials
- *If pre-traction:* Show LOIs, pilot commitments, or waitlist

## Slide 9: Competition
- 2x2 matrix or landscape chart
- X-axis: Breadth of HR coverage (single function → full department)
- Y-axis: AI nativeness (human-dependent → AI-native)
- We're in the top-right: Full coverage + AI-native
- Key differentiator: No one else offers a complete AI HR department

## Slide 10: Team
- Founder photos and bios
- Relevant experience highlights
- Key advisors (if notable)
- Why this team wins

## Slide 11: Financials & Ask
- Revenue projections (3–5 year)
- Key milestones for next 18 months
- Fundraising ask: $XXX at $XXX valuation
- Use of funds breakdown (pie chart)

## Slide 12: Vision / Closing
- The big picture: "Every company in the world will have an AI HR department"
- Contact information
- Call to action

---

## Pitch Deck Tips
- [ ] Keep it under 12 slides
- [ ] Each slide should make ONE point
- [ ] Use visuals over text
- [ ] Practice the pitch to be under 10 minutes
- [ ] Have a separate appendix with detailed financials, team bios, competitive analysis
- [ ] Design matters — use a professional template (Canva, Pitch, or hire a designer)
