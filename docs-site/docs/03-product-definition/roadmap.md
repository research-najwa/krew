## Phase 4: Scale (Year 3+)
**Goal:** Category leader, 2000+ customers, Agent Factory analyzing roles across all departments

### All 5 Divisions at Full Strength
- 18 virtual employees deployed per customer
- Full AI HR department running across all divisions
- HRBP agent handling 70%+ of HRBP workload
- Predictive analytics driving proactive workforce decisions
- Agent Factory analyzing roles beyond HR

### Agent Factory Expansion
- Analyzing JDs across all company departments (not just HR)
- Building virtual employee templates for common non-HR roles
- Secretary, financial analyst, market researcher, customer support, data entry, and more
- Companies use Agent Factory to optimize workforce composition across the entire org

### Platform Evolution
- AI-driven organizational design recommendations
- Predictive hiring (when to hire — human or agent — before managers ask)
- Industry-specific templates (healthcare HR, tech HR, retail HR)
- Marketplace for third-party agent plugins
- White-label AI HR department for consulting firms
- Unified human + AI org chart management
- Cross-division intelligence (Analytics insights auto-feed into CoE policy updates)
