# Pricing Strategy

## Pricing Model: Per-Employee-Per-Month (PEPM)

### Why PEPM?
- Aligns with HR industry standard (PEOs, EORs, HRIS all price this way)
- Scales naturally with company growth (revenue grows as customers grow)
- Easy to understand and compare
- Predictable for customers

## Proposed Pricing Tiers

### Starter — $15/employee/month
**For:** Small businesses (10–50 employees) with no HR function
**Includes:**
- Employee Relations Agent (Q&A, leave management)
- Basic Compliance Agent (policy audit, handbook generation)
- Slack + email integration
- Admin dashboard
- Email support

### Growth — $30/employee/month
**For:** Growth-stage companies (50–300 employees) building their HR function
**Includes everything in Starter, plus:**
- Onboarding Agent (full onboarding workflows)
- Recruitment Agent (JD generation, screening, scheduling)
- Performance Management Agent (reviews, goals, feedback)
- Microsoft Teams integration
- Workforce analytics dashboard
- Priority support

### Enterprise — $50/employee/month
**For:** Large companies (300+ employees) replacing or augmenting HR teams
**Includes everything in Growth, plus:**
- All 10 AI agents
- Multi-country compliance
- Custom integrations
- SSO/SAML
- Dedicated success manager
- Custom SLA
- Quarterly business reviews
- API access

## Pricing Comparison

| Solution | Cost for 100 Employees/Month |
|----------|------------------------------|
| **Our Starter** | **$1,500/month** |
| **Our Growth** | **$3,000/month** |
| **Our Enterprise** | **$5,000/month** |
| 1 HR Generalist (salary) | $5,000–$7,000/month |
| PEO (TriNet, Justworks) | $8,000–$15,000/month |
| Small HR team (3 people) | $20,000–$35,000/month |
| Full HR dept (CHRO + team) | $40,000–$70,000/month |

## Revenue Levers
1. **Base subscription** — PEPM pricing above
2. **Onboarding fee** — One-time $500–$2,000 setup and migration fee
3. **Add-on agents** — Sell individual agents à la carte for Starter tier
4. **Professional services** — Custom policy writing, compliance audits, org design
5. **Annual discount** — 20% off for annual prepayment (improves cash flow)
