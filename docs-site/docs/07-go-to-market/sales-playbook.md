# Sales Playbook

## Sales Motion

### Initial Motion: Founder-Led Sales (Months 1–12)
- Founders sell directly to first 50 customers
- Learn objections, refine pitch, understand buyer journey
- Build repeatable playbook before hiring salespeople

### Scaled Motion: Inside Sales (Month 12+)
- Hire SDRs (outbound prospecting) and AEs (closing)
- Starter tier: self-serve or low-touch
- Growth tier: inside sales (demo → trial → close)
- Enterprise tier: field sales with longer cycles

## Sales Process

```
Prospecting → Discovery Call → Demo → Trial → Proposal → Close → Onboarding
   (1 week)     (30 min)     (45 min) (14 days) (1 week)  (1 day)  (1 week)
```

**Target sales cycle:** 2–4 weeks (SMB), 4–8 weeks (mid-market), 8–16 weeks (enterprise)

## Discovery Call Framework

### Questions to Ask
1. "Tell me about your current HR setup — who handles it today?"
2. "What are your top 3 HR challenges right now?"
3. "How many HR-related tools do you use? What do you spend?"
4. "Have you had any compliance issues or close calls?"
5. "How do employees get HR support today? Are they satisfied?"
6. "What would you do if you could wave a magic wand for HR?"
7. "How comfortable is your team with AI tools?"
8. "Who would be involved in evaluating a solution like this?"

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "AI can't handle sensitive HR situations" | "You're right — that's why we have human escalation built in. Our agents handle 80% of routine work so your team (or ours) can focus on the 20% that needs a human touch." |
| "We already have HR software" | "We're not another tool — we're the team that uses the tools. Think of us as your AI HR department that integrates with your existing systems." |
| "What about data security?" | "HR data security is our #1 priority. We're SOC 2 certified, encrypt all data, and never use your data to train AI models. We can share our security whitepaper." |
| "What if the AI gives wrong advice?" | "Our agents are backed by a curated regulatory database that's continuously updated. For compliance-critical decisions, we always recommend human review, and our terms are transparent about this." |
| "We're too small / too big for this" | Small: "You're exactly who this is for — you need HR but can't afford a team." Big: "We augment your existing team — let AI handle the 70% of admin work so your HR team focuses on strategy." |

## Key Metrics
- Meetings booked per week
- Demo-to-trial conversion rate (target: 50%)
- Trial-to-paid conversion rate (target: 30%)
- Average deal size
- Sales cycle length
- Win/loss reasons
