# Go-To-Market Launch Strategy

## Launch Phases

### Phase 0: Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
**Goal:** Build awareness and waitlist before product is ready

- Build a landing page with value proposition and waitlist signup
- Start content marketing (LinkedIn, blog) about AI + HR
- Identify and engage 20 potential beta customers through personal network
- Join HR and startup communities
- Attend 2–3 relevant events/meetups
- **Target:** 500 waitlist signups, 20 warm leads

### Phase 1: Private Beta (Months 5–6)
**Goal:** Validate product with 5 paying beta customers

- Hand-pick 5 companies from waitlist (mix of sizes, industries)
- Offer 50% discount for beta participation
- White-glove onboarding (founders do setup personally)
- Weekly feedback calls
- Iterate rapidly based on feedback
- Collect testimonials and case studies
- **Target:** 5 paying customers, 80% satisfaction, 2 case studies

### Phase 2: Public Launch (Month 7)
**Goal:** Generate awareness and first wave of self-serve signups

- Product Hunt launch
- Press coverage (TechCrunch, HR tech publications)
- Launch announcement on social media
- Email blast to waitlist
- Founder-led LinkedIn content campaign
- Webinar: "The Future of AI-Powered HR"
- **Target:** 200 signups, 20 paying customers

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 8–18)
**Goal:** Repeatable sales motion, 100+ customers

- Hire first 2 salespeople
- Build outbound sales playbook
- Scale content marketing and SEO
- Launch partner program
- Start paid advertising (LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads)
- Conference presence (HR Tech Conference, etc.)
- **Target:** 100 paying customers, $3M ARR

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Phase | Expected CAC | Volume |
|---------|-------|-------------|--------|
| Founder network | Beta | $0 | Low |
| Content marketing (LinkedIn, blog) | Beta+ | $2,000 | Medium |
| Product Hunt | Launch | $500 | Spike |
| SEO / inbound | Month 6+ | $3,000 | Growing |
| Outbound sales | Month 8+ | $8,000 | High |
| Partnerships (VCs, accountants) | Month 6+ | $1,500 | Medium |
| Paid ads (LinkedIn, Google) | Month 10+ | $5,000 | Scalable |
| Events / conferences | Ongoing | $10,000 | Low but high-value |
