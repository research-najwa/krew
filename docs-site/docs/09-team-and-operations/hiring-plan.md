## Key Hiring Principles
1. **Hire generalists early** — People who can wear multiple hats
2. **AI expertise is critical** — First 2–3 engineers must be strong in LLM/AI
3. **Domain expertise through advisors** — Don't need to hire full-time HR expert immediately
4. **Remote-first** — Access global talent, reduce costs
5. **Equity-heavy compensation early** — Preserve cash, attract believers
