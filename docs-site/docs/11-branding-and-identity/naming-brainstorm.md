# Company Naming & Domain

## Naming Criteria
- Short (1–2 words, max 3 syllables)
- Easy to spell and pronounce
- Available as .com domain
- Not too similar to existing HR/AI companies
- Conveys: modern, AI, trust, people
- Works globally (no negative meanings in other languages)

- [ ] Check trademark databases (USPTO, SAIP)
- [ ] Check for negative meanings in other languages
