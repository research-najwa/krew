# Data Protection & Privacy Compliance

## Key Regulations by Region

| Regulation | Region | Key Requirements | Priority |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|----------|
| **GDPR** | EU/EEA | Consent, DPA, right to erasure, 72hr breach notification | High |
| **PDPL** | Saudi Arabia | Similar to GDPR, data localization requirements | High |
| **CCPA/CPRA** | California | Consumer rights, opt-out of data sale | High |
| **PIPEDA** | Canada | Consent-based, breach reporting | Medium |
| **UAE Data Protection** | UAE | Federal Decree-Law No. 45, data localization | High |
| **UK GDPR** | UK | Post-Brexit GDPR equivalent | Medium |

## Core Requirements (Applies to All)

### 1. Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)
- [ ] Draft standard DPA for all customers
- [ ] Define roles: We are the "data processor"; customer is "data controller"
- [ ] List all sub-processors (LLM providers, cloud, etc.)
- [ ] Sub-processor notification process

### 2. Consent & Lawful Basis
- Employee data processed under "legitimate interest" or "contractual necessity"
- Clear privacy notices for employees of customer companies
- Opt-in/opt-out mechanisms where required

### 3. Data Subject Rights
Must support:
- Right to access (employee can see their data)
- Right to rectification (employee can correct data)
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to data portability (export in standard format)
- Right to object to processing

### 4. Data Retention
- Define retention periods for each data type
- Automated deletion after retention period
- Customer can request full data deletion on contract end

### 5. AI-Specific Considerations
- Transparency: Employees must know they're interacting with AI
- Explainability: AI decisions (especially in hiring, performance) must be explainable
- Human oversight: Significant HR decisions require human review
- Bias monitoring: Regular audits for discriminatory patterns
- [ ] Research EU AI Act implications for HR AI
