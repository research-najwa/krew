    AI Agents Catalog — AI HR Department¶

    Overview¶

    Our platform delivers a complete AI-powered HR department structured around the proven Ulrich HR Operating Model. We build AI agents across five divisions — the four traditional HR pillars plus the Agent Factory, which is the division responsible for workforce composition decisions (human vs. agent).

    Division 1: HR Centers of Excellence (CoE)¶

    What HR CoEs Do¶

    Centers of Excellence are specialist teams that design policies, programs, and best-practice frameworks for the organization. They don't handle day-to-day execution (that's HR Shared Services) — they design the systems and standards.

    CoE Agents¶

    Talent Acquisition CoE Agent¶

    Replaces: TA Strategy Lead / Head of Recruiting ($90K–$140K) Division role: Designs the recruitment strategy, frameworks, and standards

    Capabilities¶

    • Design and optimize the company's end-to-end recruitment process
    • Build competency frameworks and job architecture
    • Define interview methodologies and scoring rubrics
    • Create employer branding strategy and messaging
    • Set up structured hiring playbooks by role type and level
    • Benchmark hiring metrics against industry standards
    • Define sourcing channel strategy and budget allocation
    • Design assessment criteria and evaluation frameworks
    • Create offer approval workflows and compensation bands for new roles

    Outputs¶

    • Recruitment playbook
    • Interview guides by role family
    • Employer brand guidelines
    • Sourcing strategy documents
    • Hiring metrics benchmarks

    Compensation & Benefits CoE Agent¶

    Replaces: Total Rewards Manager / C&B Specialist ($75K–$120K) Division role: Designs the company's compensation philosophy, pay structures, and benefits strategy

    Capabilities¶

    • Design compensation philosophy and strategy documents
    • Build and maintain salary band structures by level, function, and geography
    • Conduct market benchmarking studies using industry data
    • Design benefits package strategy (health, retirement, perks)
    • Create compensation review cycle frameworks
    • Build equity/stock compensation models
    • Analyze pay equity and design remediation plans
    • Design incentive and bonus structures
    • Create pay transparency compliance frameworks

    Outputs¶

    • Compensation philosophy document
    • Salary band matrix
    • Benefits strategy
    • Pay equity audit reports
    • Annual compensation review framework

    Learning & Development CoE Agent¶

    Replaces: L&D Manager / Talent Development Lead ($70K–$110K) Division role: Designs the learning strategy, competency frameworks, and development programs

    Capabilities¶

    • Build competency models and skills taxonomies
    • Design learning paths by role, level, and career track
    • Create leadership development frameworks
    • Design onboarding curriculum architecture
    • Build mentorship and coaching program structures
    • Design compliance training programs
    • Create skills assessment frameworks
    • Evaluate and recommend learning technology and content providers
    • Measure learning ROI methodology

    Outputs¶

    • Competency framework
    • Learning path catalog
    • Leadership development program design
    • Skills gap analysis reports

    Compliance & Policy CoE Agent¶

    Replaces: HR Compliance Manager / Employment Law Specialist ($80K–$130K) Division role: Designs policies, ensures regulatory compliance, and manages the legal framework of employment

    Capabilities¶

    • Draft and maintain all company HR policies and the employee handbook
    • Monitor labor law changes across all jurisdictions in real time
    • Conduct compliance gap audits against current regulations
    • Design mandatory training programs (harassment, safety, ethics)
    • Create work authorization and visa management frameworks
    • Build compliance reporting templates for audits
    • Design whistleblower and grievance policies
    • Create data privacy frameworks for employee data (GDPR, PDPL)
    • Advise on worker classification (employee vs. contractor)

    Outputs¶

    • Employee handbook (auto-updated)
    • Policy library
    • Compliance audit reports
    • Regulatory change alerts
    • Training requirement matrices by jurisdiction

    Performance Management CoE Agent¶

    Replaces: Performance & OD Manager ($75K–$115K) Division role: Designs the performance management system, goal frameworks, and review processes

    Capabilities¶

    • Design performance management philosophy and frameworks (OKRs, MBOs, etc.)
    • Build performance review cycle architecture (cadence, participants, criteria)
    • Create 360-degree feedback program designs
    • Design calibration and talent review processes
    • Build performance improvement plan (PIP) frameworks
    • Design promotion criteria and career leveling frameworks
    • Create manager effectiveness evaluation systems
    • Design recognition and reward program structures

    Outputs¶

    • Performance review templates
    • Calibration process guide
    • Career ladder / leveling framework
    • PIP templates and guidelines
    • Recognition program design

    Culture & Engagement CoE Agent¶

    Replaces: Culture & Employee Experience Lead ($70K–$110K) Division role: Designs the engagement strategy, culture programs, and employee experience frameworks

    Capabilities¶

    • Design employee engagement survey strategy and questions
    • Build culture values framework and activation plans
    • Create employee recognition program architecture
    • Design DEI strategy and initiatives
    • Build employee experience journey maps
    • Create internal communication frameworks
    • Design wellbeing and mental health program structures
    • Create team building and social connection programs
    • Design change management communication plans

    Outputs¶

    • Engagement survey framework
    • Culture playbook
    • DEI strategy document
    • Employee experience maps
    • Recognition program guidelines

    Division 2: HR Business Partners (HRBP)¶

    What HRBPs Do¶

    HRBPs are strategic advisors embedded in business units who translate business needs into people strategy. They sit at the intersection of business and HR, working directly with leaders.

    HRBP Agent¶

    Replaces: HR Business Partner ($90K–$150K) Mode: HYBRID — agent handles 60-70% (analysis, recommendations, preparation, execution of routine items), human handles 30-40% (sensitive conversations, final strategic decisions, relationship building)

    What the Agent Handles (60-70%)¶

    • Analyze business unit workforce data and surface insights to managers
    • Prepare workforce planning recommendations (headcount, skills gaps, succession)
    • Draft talking points and recommendations for manager 1:1s
    • Generate organizational health reports per business unit
    • Model scenarios: "What happens if we restructure team X?"
    • Track and analyze team-level engagement, performance, and retention metrics
    • Prepare talent review materials (9-box, succession pipelines)
    • Provide managers with real-time coaching prompts and suggestions
    • Execute approved HR actions (process promotions, transfers, comp changes)
    • Surface early warning signals (attrition risk, engagement drops, performance trends)
    • Benchmark business unit HR metrics against internal and industry standards
    • Prepare board and leadership people reports for business units

    What Requires Human (30-40%)¶

    • Sensitive employee relations conversations (terminations, grievances, investigations)
    • Coaching senior leaders through complex organizational changes
    • Navigating political dynamics and relationship building
    • Final approval on strategic workforce decisions
    • Facilitating difficult team dynamics
    • Executive-level presentations and influence

    HRBP Agent KPIs¶

    • Insights delivered per business unit per month
    • Manager adoption rate of recommendations
    • Time saved for managers on people decisions
    • Business unit employee engagement scores
    • Escalation quality (correct routing, appropriate escalation)

    Escalation Rules¶

    • Harassment, discrimination, or legal claims → immediate human + legal
    • Termination decisions → always human-in-the-loop
    • Organizational restructuring → human approves, agent executes
    • Sensitive interpersonal conflicts → human handles directly

    Division 3: HR Analytics & Finance¶

    What HR Analytics & Finance Does¶

    HR Analytics & Finance is the data intelligence and financial planning layer that turns people data into actionable insights, predictions, and financial decisions. It feeds insights to all other divisions and bridges HR with the CFO/Finance function.

    Analytics & Finance Agents¶

    People Analytics Agent¶

    Replaces: People Analytics Analyst / HR Data Analyst ($80K–$120K) Division role: Core analytics engine — dashboards, reports, and descriptive analytics

    Capabilities¶

    • Build and maintain real-time HR metrics dashboards
    • Headcount, turnover, retention, time-to-fill, cost-per-hire
    • Diversity metrics (gender, ethnicity, age, geography)
    • Engagement scores and trends
    • Compensation distribution and equity analysis
    • Training completion and effectiveness
    • Generate automated monthly/quarterly HR reports
    • Slice and dice data by department, location, level, tenure, manager
    • Answer ad-hoc data questions from HR and business leaders
    • Track HR OKRs and KPIs with alerting
    • Benchmark metrics against industry peers

    Outputs¶

    • Executive HR dashboards
    • Monthly people reports
    • Board-level workforce summaries
    • Custom analytics on demand

    Predictive Workforce Intelligence Agent¶

    Replaces: Senior People Analytics / Workforce Planning Analyst ($100K–$140K) Division role: Predictive and prescriptive analytics — what will happen and what to do about it

    Capabilities¶

    • Attrition prediction — identify employees at risk of leaving (and why) before they resign
    • Hiring demand forecasting — predict when departments will need new headcount based on growth, seasonality, and project pipelines
    • Skills gap forecasting — identify emerging skills needs based on business strategy and market trends
    • Succession planning intelligence — identify readiness and risk in leadership pipeline
    • Engagement early warning — detect engagement drops before they show up in surveys
    • Compensation competitiveness alerts — flag when roles are falling below market
    • Organizational network analysis — map influence and collaboration patterns
    • DEI trend forecasting — project diversity pipeline and representation trends
    • Virtual workforce optimization — recommend which additional roles to convert from human to agent

    Outputs¶

    • Attrition risk reports with recommended actions
    • Workforce planning models
    • Skills gap analysis
    • Succession pipeline health reports
    • Predictive engagement alerts

    Workforce Budget & Planning Agent¶

    Replaces: HR Finance Analyst / Workforce Planning Analyst ($75K–$110K) Division role: Manage workforce budgets, cost modeling, and financial planning

    Capabilities¶

    • Budget Management
    • Build and manage annual workforce/HR budgets
    • Track budget vs. actuals in real time by department, location, and level
    • Alert when departments are trending over budget

    • Model headcount costs including fully-loaded expenses (salary + benefits + taxes + overhead)

    • Cost Modeling

    • Model total cost of compensation changes (raises, promotions, bonuses)
    • Scenario planning: "What if we give 5% raises vs. 3%?"
    • Model cost impact of new hires before approval
    • Calculate cost of vacancy (revenue impact of unfilled roles)

    • Compare virtual employee costs vs. human equivalents per role

    • Financial Reporting

    • Generate monthly HR financial reports for CFO/leadership
    • Headcount reports with fully-loaded costs
    • Benefits utilization and cost analysis
    • Overtime and contractor spending analysis
    • Board-level workforce financial summaries

    Outputs¶

    • Monthly workforce cost reports
    • Budget variance analysis
    • Headcount cost models
    • Scenario analysis decks

    HR ROI & Financial Analytics Agent¶

    Replaces: Senior Finance Analyst (HR focus) ($85K–$120K) Division role: Measure the financial return of HR initiatives and the virtual workforce

    Capabilities¶

    • HR Initiative ROI
    • Calculate cost-per-hire across channels and departments
    • Measure ROI of training and L&D programs
    • Track turnover cost (replacement cost, lost productivity, ramp time)
    • Benchmark HR spending ratios against industry peers

    • Calculate ROI of engagement and retention programs

    • Virtual Workforce ROI

    • Calculate ROI of each virtual employee vs. human equivalent
    • Track cumulative savings from agent deployment
    • Model ROI of expanding agent deployment to additional roles

    • Generate business cases for new agent deployments

    • Predictive Finance

    • Forecast future workforce costs based on growth plans
    • Model financial impact of attrition scenarios
    • Predict benefits cost trends

    • Budget impact of regulatory changes (minimum wage, benefits mandates)

    • Payroll Analytics

    • Analyze payroll accuracy and error rates
    • Track payroll tax compliance across jurisdictions
    • Identify payroll cost optimization opportunities
    • Benchmark payroll costs as % of revenue

    Integrations¶

    • Accounting systems (QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite)
    • Payroll systems (ADP, Gusto, Deel)
    • ERP systems (SAP, Oracle)
    • Financial planning tools (Anaplan, Adaptive)

    Outputs¶

    • HR ROI dashboards
    • Virtual workforce savings reports
    • Financial forecast models
    • Cost optimization recommendations

    Division 4: HR Shared Services¶

    What HR Shared Services Does¶

    HR Shared Services is the high-volume, transactional operations engine that handles day-to-day HR execution. This is where most employee touchpoints happen. It's the division with the highest volume, highest cost, and highest automation potential — making it the priority for AI deployment.

    Shared Services Agents¶

    Employee Services Agent¶

    Replaces: HR Coordinator / HR Service Desk ($45K–$65K) Division role: Frontline employee support — the "help desk" of HR

    Capabilities¶

    • Answer employee questions about policies, PTO, benefits, payroll 24/7
    • Process leave requests (sick, vacation, parental, bereavement)
    • Handle address/name/bank changes and personal information updates
    • Process employment verification requests
    • Manage time and attendance tracking
    • Handle basic accommodation requests
    • Route complex issues to the appropriate specialist or HRBP
    • Document all interactions for compliance audit trail

    KPIs¶

    • Tickets resolved per day
    • First-response time (<30 seconds)
    • Resolution rate without escalation (target: 85%+)
    • Employee satisfaction score (CSAT)

    Onboarding Operations Agent¶

    Replaces: Onboarding Coordinator ($45K–$60K) Division role: Execute the end-to-end onboarding process for every new hire

    Capabilities¶

    • Generate personalized onboarding checklists based on role, department, location
    • Collect and verify new hire documents (ID, tax forms, bank details, NDAs)
    • Coordinate IT provisioning (accounts, hardware, access)
    • Schedule orientation sessions and buddy assignments
    • Deliver company policy and compliance training
    • Conduct day-1, week-1, 30/60/90-day structured check-ins
    • Gather onboarding feedback and flag issues to HRBP
    • Track onboarding completion metrics and time-to-productivity

    KPIs¶

    • Onboarding completion rate (100% target)
    • Time-to-productivity reduction
    • New hire satisfaction score
    • Document collection completion time

    Recruitment Operations Agent¶

    Replaces: Recruitment Coordinator / Junior Recruiter ($50K–$80K) Division role: Execute the recruitment process designed by the TA CoE

    Capabilities¶

    • Write and post job descriptions (using CoE frameworks and templates)
    • Screen and rank resumes against role criteria
    • Conduct initial screening conversations (text/voice)
    • Schedule interviews with hiring managers and panels
    • Send candidate status updates and communications
    • Collect interviewer feedback and generate scorecards
    • Run reference checks (automated outreach)
    • Generate offer letters using compensation band approvals
    • Track pipeline metrics (time-to-fill, source effectiveness, funnel conversion)
    • Manage candidate experience end-to-end

    KPIs¶

    • Time-to-fill per role
    • Candidate experience score
    • Screening accuracy (quality of shortlist)
    • Pipeline velocity

    Payroll Operations Agent¶

    Replaces: Payroll Specialist / Payroll Coordinator ($50K–$75K) Division role: Execute payroll processing accurately and on time

    Capabilities¶

    • Process monthly/bi-weekly payroll calculations
    • Handle tax withholdings and filings by jurisdiction
    • Process salary adjustments, bonuses, and commissions
    • Manage benefits deductions and enrollment changes
    • Process expense reimbursements
    • Generate pay stubs and tax documents (W-2, 1099, etc.)
    • Track PTO balances and accruals
    • Reconcile payroll reports with finance
    • Flag payroll anomalies for review

    KPIs¶

    • Payroll accuracy rate (99.9% target)
    • On-time processing rate
    • Error correction turnaround time
    • Employee payroll inquiry resolution time

    Offboarding Operations Agent¶

    Replaces: HR Coordinator (exit process) ($45K–$60K) Division role: Execute employee offboarding end-to-end

    Capabilities¶

    • Generate offboarding checklists customized by role and departure type
    • Conduct exit interviews (conversational AI)
    • Process final pay, PTO payout, and benefits continuation (COBRA)
    • Coordinate IT access revocation and equipment return
    • Handle knowledge transfer documentation
    • Generate separation agreements and NDAs
    • Route exit interview insights to HR Analytics for trend analysis
    • Manage alumni network communications

    KPIs¶

    • Offboarding checklist completion rate
    • Exit interview completion rate
    • IT access revocation time (<24 hours)
    • Knowledge transfer documentation quality

    Employee Records & Compliance Operations Agent¶

    Replaces: HR Administrator / Records Specialist ($40K–$55K) Division role: Maintain accurate employee data and ensure operational compliance

    Capabilities¶

    • Maintain employee master data (personal info, job history, compensation)
    • Track mandatory training completion and send reminders
    • Manage document retention and archiving per policy
    • Process work permit and visa renewals
    • Track contract renewals and probation periods
    • Generate compliance reports for audits
    • Ensure data accuracy across all HR systems

    KPIs¶

    • Data accuracy rate (99%+ target)
    • Compliance training completion rate
    • Audit readiness score
    • Document processing time

    Division 5: Agent Factory¶

    What the Agent Factory Does¶

    The Agent Factory is the workforce composition division within HR. When a company needs to fill a role, the Agent Factory analyzes the job description and decides: should this role be a human or a virtual employee? If an agent can do the job, the factory deploys one. If not, it routes the role to recruiting.

    This is a new HR function that doesn't exist in traditional organizations — it's what makes an AI HR department fundamentally different.

    Agent Factory Engine¶

    How It Works¶

    INPUT: Job Description (any role the company wants to fill)
  ↓
STEP 1: Parse the JD into structured components
  - Core responsibilities and daily tasks
  - Required skills and judgment level
  - Human interaction requirements (internal, external, sensitive)
  - Decision-making authority
  - Creative vs. process-driven work ratio
  - Regulatory/legal implications
  ↓
STEP 2: Score each dimension on an "AI Readiness" scale
  - Task repeatability:        [Low ←──→ High]
  - Rule-based vs. judgment:   [Judgment ←──→ Rule-based]
  - Human empathy required:    [High ←──→ Low]
  - Data availability:         [Low ←──→ High]
  - Error tolerance:           [Zero ←──→ Flexible]
  - Compliance sensitivity:    [High ←──→ Low]
  ↓
STEP 3: Generate recommendation
  - 🟢 FULL AGENT: AI agent can do 90%+ of this role → Deploy virtual employee
  - 🟡 HYBRID: AI agent can handle 50-90% → Deploy agent + human oversight
  - 🔴 HUMAN NEEDED: Role requires >50% human judgment → Route to Recruitment Ops
  ↓
STEP 4: Configure and deploy (if agent)
  - Create virtual employee profile (name, role, team, KPIs)
  - Connect to relevant systems and communication channels
  - Set escalation rules and human oversight parameters
  - Deploy to the customer's workspace

    Every JD analyzed makes the factory smarter: - Builds proprietary dataset of "what can be automated" - Improves accuracy of human-vs-agent recommendations - Identifies new agent templates to build - Benchmarks across industries, company sizes, and role types

    How the Divisions Work Together¶

    EXAMPLE: A new employee joins the company

CoE (designed the process)
  → Talent Acquisition CoE defined the hiring framework
  → Compensation CoE set the salary bands
  → L&D CoE designed the onboarding curriculum
  → Compliance CoE ensured the offer letter is compliant

Agent Factory (decided human vs. agent)
  → Manager submitted a JD for the role
  → Factory analyzed it and recommended: hire a human
  → Routed to Recruitment Ops

HRBP (strategic oversight)
  → HRBP Agent identified the headcount need with the business unit leader
  → Approved the role and compensation level

Analytics & Finance (informed the decisions + tracked costs)
  → Predicted the team needs a new hire based on workload data
  → Benchmarked the compensation against market
  → Modeled the fully-loaded cost before approval
  → Updated the department budget

Shared Services (executed the work)
  → Recruitment Ops Agent screened candidates and scheduled interviews
  → Employee Services Agent answered the new hire's pre-start questions
  → Onboarding Ops Agent collected documents and set up day-1
  → Payroll Ops Agent processed the first paycheck
