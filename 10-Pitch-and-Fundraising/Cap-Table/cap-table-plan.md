## Key Principles
1. **Use Carta or Pulley** for cap table management from day one
2. **Always have an option pool** before raising (investors expect it)
3. **All founders vest** — protects everyone
4. **83(b) election** — File within 30 days of receiving shares (US tax optimization)
5. **Keep it simple** — Avoid complex share classes early on
