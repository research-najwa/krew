# Channel Partnership Strategy

## Partnership Tiers

### Tier 1: Referral Partners
- **Who:** Accountants, business consultants, startup advisors
- **How:** They refer clients to us; we pay 10–20% of first-year revenue
- **Effort:** Low — just needs referral tracking and payout
- **Target:** 20 referral partners in Year 1

### Tier 2: Integration Partners
- **Who:** Payroll providers, ATS companies, benefits platforms
- **How:** Bidirectional integration; co-marketing; joint customers
- **Effort:** Medium — requires engineering + BD work
- **Target:** 5 integration partners at launch

### Tier 3: Strategic / Reseller Partners
- **Who:** HR consulting firms, PEO/EOR companies, system integrators
- **How:** White-label or reseller agreement; they sell our platform under their brand or alongside their services
- **Effort:** High — requires dedicated partner success team
- **Target:** 2–3 strategic partners in Year 1

## Partner Program (Phase 2)
- Partner portal with co-branded materials
- Deal registration and pipeline visibility
- Tiered commission structure (Silver, Gold, Platinum)
- Joint webinars and case studies
- Partner certification program
