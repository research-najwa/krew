# Content Strategy

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: "The AI HR Revolution" (Thought Leadership)
- Why AI will transform HR
- What the HR department of the future looks like
- Case for AI agents over traditional HR software
- **Goal:** Position founders as visionaries in AI HR

### Pillar 2: "HR Done Right" (Educational)
- How to handle common HR situations
- Compliance guides by jurisdiction
- Employee experience best practices
- Onboarding checklists and templates
- **Goal:** SEO traffic, build trust, attract target audience

### Pillar 3: "Customer Stories" (Social Proof)
- Before/after case studies
- ROI reports from real customers
- Employee testimonials
- **Goal:** Drive conversions

### Pillar 4: "Product & Innovation" (Product Marketing)
- New agent launches and feature updates
- How our agents work (behind the scenes)
- Integration announcements
- **Goal:** Educate prospects, retain customers
