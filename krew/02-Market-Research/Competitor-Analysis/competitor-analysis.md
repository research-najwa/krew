# Competitor Analysis

## Competitive Landscape Map

| Company | What They Do | AI Strategy | Our Advantage |
|---------|-------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Workday** | Enterprise HCM suite | Adding AI copilots to existing platform | They're bolting AI onto legacy; we're AI-native |

## Key Insight
**No one is building a "full-stack AI HR department."** Everyone is either:
- A traditional HR platform adding AI features (incremental)
- An AI tool solving ONE HR problem (recruiting, helpdesk, etc.)
- An outsourcing company using humans (expensive)

**Our white space: The complete AI-native HR function as a subscription service.**
