# Market Sizing: TAM, SAM, SOM

## Approach: Bottom-Up Estimation

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
**"If every company in the world used AI agents for HR, how big is the market?"**

| Input | Estimate | Source |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Global companies with 10+ employees | ~30 million | [ ] Research |
| Average employees per company (10+) | ~100 | [ ] Research |
| Total addressable employees | ~3 billion | Estimate |
| Revenue per employee per month | $15–$50 | Pricing assumption |
| **Annual TAM** | **$540B–$1.8T** | Calculated |

> Note: This is theoretical maximum. Refine with real data.

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
**"Realistically, which segments can we serve with our product in the next 3–5 years?"**

| Filter | Estimate |
|--------|----------|
| Geography: English-speaking + GCC markets | ~5 million companies |
| Company size: 10–5,000 employees | ~4 million companies |
| Tech adoption readiness (exclude very traditional) | ~2 million companies |
| Average employees per company | ~150 |
| Total addressable employees | ~300 million |
| Revenue per employee per month | $20 (blended) |
| **Annual SAM** | **~$72B** |
