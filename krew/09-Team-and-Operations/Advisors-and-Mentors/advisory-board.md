# Advisory Board Plan

## Advisor Roles Needed

| Expertise | Why | Compensation | Target Profile |
|-----------|-----|-------------|---------------|
| **HR Domain Expert** | Validate product, open doors to customers | 0.25–0.5% equity | Former CHRO at 500+ employee company |
| **AI/ML Technical Advisor** | Architecture review, model selection | 0.25–0.5% equity | Senior AI engineer or AI startup founder |
| **SaaS Go-To-Market** | Sales strategy, pricing, scaling | 0.25–0.5% equity | VP Sales or CEO of successful B2B SaaS |
| **Legal / Compliance** | Employment law, data privacy | 0.25% equity or hourly | Employment attorney or compliance officer |
| **Regional Market Expert** | Local regulations, market entry | 0.25% equity | Business leader in target geography |

## Advisor Agreement Terms (Standard)
- Equity: 0.25–0.5% per advisor
- Vesting: 2-year vesting, monthly
- Time commitment: 2–4 hours/month
- Duration: 1–2 year term, renewable
- Use FAST Agreement (Founder/Advisor Standard Template)

## Where to Find Advisors
- [ ] Personal and professional network
- [ ] LinkedIn outreach to target profiles
- [ ] Industry events and conferences
- [ ] Accelerator mentor networks
- [ ] AngelList advisor matching
