# Terms, Policies & Legal Documents Checklist

## Customer-Facing Documents
- [ ] **Terms of Service** — Contract governing use of platform
- [ ] **Privacy Policy** — How we handle data
- [ ] **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** — GDPR/PDPL compliance
- [ ] **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** — Uptime, response times, support
- [ ] **Acceptable Use Policy** — What customers can/cannot do
- [ ] **Security Whitepaper** — Technical security overview for enterprise sales

## Internal Documents
- [ ] **Employee Handbook** (our own company)
- [ ] **IP Assignment Agreement** — All team members assign IP to company
- [ ] **NDA / Confidentiality Agreement** — For employees, contractors, advisors
- [ ] **Equity Agreements** — Stock option plan, grant agreements

## Customer Contract Structure
- Master Subscription Agreement (MSA)
- Order Form (specifies tier, employee count, pricing)
- DPA (data protection addendum)
- SLA (service level addendum)

## Questions
- [ ] Do we need E&O (Errors & Omissions) insurance?
- [ ] Cyber liability insurance?
- [ ] At what stage do we need in-house counsel vs. outside firm?
