# Company Registration Checklist

## Decision: Where to Incorporate?

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Delaware C-Corp (US)** | Standard for VC-backed startups, investor-friendly, strong legal framework | US tax obligations, foreign qualification if operating elsewhere |
| **Saudi Arabia** | Local market advantage, Vision 2030 incentives, MISA support | Less familiar to global VCs, evolving corporate law |
| **UAE (DIFC or ADGM)** | Tax-friendly, English common law, regional hub | Smaller market, less VC infrastructure |
| **Cayman/BVI + Operating Entity** | Tax-efficient holding structure | Complexity, cost |

## Recommended: Delaware C-Corp + Local Operating Entity
- Incorporate parent company in Delaware (standard for raising US VC)
- Set up operating entity in primary market (Saudi/UAE) for local operations
- [ ] Consult with startup attorney to confirm structure

## Registration Steps

### US (Delaware C-Corp)
- [ ] Choose company name (check availability)
- [ ] File Certificate of Incorporation with Delaware Secretary of State
- [ ] Obtain EIN (Employer Identification Number) from IRS
- [ ] Open US business bank account (Mercury, SVB, etc.)
- [ ] Draft and adopt bylaws
- [ ] Issue founder shares (83(b) election if applicable)
- [ ] Set up cap table (Carta, Pulley, or Angelist Stack)
- [ ] Foreign qualify in state of operations (if not Delaware)

### Saudi Arabia (if applicable)
- [ ] Register with MISA (Ministry of Investment)
- [ ] Obtain Commercial Registration (CR)
- [ ] Register with GOSI (social insurance)
- [ ] Obtain Saudization certificate
- [ ] Open local bank account

### Legal Documents Needed
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation
- [ ] Bylaws
- [ ] Founders' Agreement (equity split, vesting, roles, IP assignment)
- [ ] Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement
- [ ] Confidentiality / NDA template
- [ ] Employee offer letter template
- [ ] Contractor agreement template
- [ ] Privacy Policy
- [ ] Terms of Service
