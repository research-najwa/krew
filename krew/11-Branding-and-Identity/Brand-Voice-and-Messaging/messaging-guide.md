# Brand Voice & Messaging Guide

## Brand Voice

### We Are:
- **Clear** — No jargon, no buzzwords. Say what we mean.
- **Confident** — We believe AI can do HR better. We say so.
- **Empathetic** — We understand HR is about people, not just processes.
- **Practical** — We focus on outcomes, not hype.

### We Are NOT:
- **Robotic** — Even though we're AI, we don't sound like robots
- **Corporate** — No "synergize," "leverage," or "paradigm shift"
- **Arrogant** — We acknowledge what AI can't do (yet)
- **Vague** — We use specific numbers and examples

## Key Messages

### Primary Message
"We build AI agents that run your entire HR department — better, faster, and at a fraction of the cost."

### For Different Audiences

**To CEOs/Founders:**
"Stop worrying about HR. Our AI agents handle everything from hiring to compliance, so you can focus on building your business."

**To CFOs:**
"Replace $500K in HR costs with a $3K/month subscription. Same coverage, better consistency, zero hiring headaches."

**To HR Leaders:**
"Let AI handle the 70% of HR that's administrative. Focus your team on the strategic work that actually moves the needle."

**To Employees:**
"Get instant answers to any HR question, 24/7. Request time off, understand your benefits, or get onboarding help — all in Slack."

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"We're building the first AI company that replaces your entire HR department. Instead of hiring an HR team or outsourcing to an expensive PEO, companies subscribe to our AI agents that handle everything — recruiting, onboarding, compliance, employee support, performance management. It's enterprise-grade HR for a startup budget."
