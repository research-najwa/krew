## The Agent Factory Division
One of the five divisions in our AI HR department. When a company needs to fill any role, the Agent Factory analyzes the job description and decides: **human or agent?**

1. **A company submits a job description** (any role — secretary, financial analyst, market researcher, HR coordinator, etc.)
2. **The Agent Factory analyzes the role** — tasks, skills, judgment required, human interaction level
3. **It decides:** Can an AI agent do this job? Fully? Partially? Or does it require a human?
4. **If an agent can do it:** Deploy a virtual employee with a name, role, KPIs, and communication channels
5. **If it needs a human:** Route to the Recruitment Operations Agent in HR Shared Services to find the right person

## Core Beliefs
1. **HR is essential but broken** — Most companies either can't afford a full HR function or suffer from inconsistent, outdated HR practices.
2. **Not every job needs a human** — Many knowledge-work roles are process-heavy and rule-based. AI agents can do them better, cheaper, and 24/7.
3. **AI can deliver consistent excellence** — Unlike human teams that vary in quality, AI agents deliver best-practice execution every single time.
4. **The future of work is hybrid: humans + agents** — Companies will have org charts with both human and virtual employees. We build the platform that makes this possible.
5. **People operations should be proactive, not reactive** — AI enables a shift from firefighting to strategic workforce planning.
6. **Compliance shouldn't require an army of lawyers** — AI agents can stay current with regulations across jurisdictions automatically.
7. **Every employee deserves a great HR experience** — Regardless of their employer's size or budget.
