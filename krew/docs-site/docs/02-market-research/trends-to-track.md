# HR Industry Trends to Track

## Macro Trends

1. **AI Agent Economy** — Shift from chatbots to autonomous AI agents that execute tasks
2. **Remote-First Work** — Companies hiring globally, need digital HR infrastructure
3. **Skills-Based Hiring** — Moving from degrees/titles to skills-based talent management
4. **Employee Experience (EX)** — HR shifting from admin function to employee-centric
5. **People Analytics** — Data-driven workforce decisions becoming standard
6. **Continuous Performance Management** — Annual reviews dying, replaced by ongoing feedback
7. **Mental Health & Wellbeing** — Growing employer responsibility for employee wellness
8. **Gig Economy / Contractor Management** — Blurring lines between employees and contractors
9. **DEI Evolution** — Diversity, equity, and inclusion as strategic priorities
10. **Labor Law Complexity** — Regulations increasing globally, especially around AI and remote work
