## Content Marketing Strategy

### Thought Leadership Topics
1. The future of AI in HR
2. How AI agents are replacing traditional HR departments
3. Compliance automation — why AI gets it right
4. Employee experience in the age of AI
5. The real cost of bad HR (for SMBs)
6. AI bias in hiring — how to get it right

### Content Calendar (Weekly Cadence)
| Day | Content Type | Channel |
|-----|-------------|---------|
| Monday | LinkedIn post (founder insight) | LinkedIn |
| Tuesday | Blog article (SEO-optimized) | Company blog |
| Wednesday | LinkedIn post (product feature or customer story) | LinkedIn |
| Thursday | Short video or infographic | LinkedIn + Twitter/X |
| Friday | Newsletter (weekly HR + AI digest) | Email |

### Content Formats
- Blog posts (1,500–2,500 words, SEO-focused)
- LinkedIn posts (personal brand of founders)
- Case studies (customer success stories)
- Webinars (monthly)
- Whitepapers / guides (quarterly)
- Short videos (product demos, explainers)
- Podcast appearances (guest on HR and AI podcasts)
