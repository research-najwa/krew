# Visual Identity & Brand Guidelines

## Brand Personality
- **Professional** — We handle sensitive HR data; trust is essential
- **Modern** — AI-native, not legacy software
- **Approachable** — Employees interact with our agents daily; shouldn't feel cold
- **Confident** — We replace entire departments; need to project capability
- **Human** — Despite being AI, our brand should feel warm and human-centric

## Color Palette Direction
| Mood | Primary | Secondary | Accent |
|------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Trust + Modern | Deep blue (#1a365d) | Light gray (#f7fafc) | Teal (#38b2ac) |
| Warm + Tech | Indigo (#5046e5) | Warm white (#fefcfb) | Coral (#f56565) |
| Bold + Clean | Black (#1a202c) | White (#ffffff) | Electric blue (#3b82f6) |

> [ ] Choose direction after naming is finalized

## Typography
- **Headings:** Modern sans-serif (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or custom)
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, system fonts)
- **Code/Data:** Monospace for dashboards

## Logo Requirements
- Works at small sizes (favicon, app icon)
- Looks good in single color (black/white)
- Memorable and distinct
- Conveys AI + people/HR
- [ ] Hire designer or use design agency
