# Value Proposition

## One-Liner
**"A complete AI-powered HR department — subscribe and deploy."**

## The Core Value: A Full HR Department, Powered by AI
Most companies either can't afford a proper HR function or suffer from inconsistent, understaffed HR teams. We deliver a **complete AI HR department** with 18 virtual employees across 5 divisions — for 90% less than traditional HR staffing.

Our HR department includes an **Agent Factory** division that analyzes any job description and tells you: does this role need a human, or can a virtual employee do it?

## Value Proposition Canvas

### Customer Jobs (What companies need)
1. Hire the right people, fast
2. Run day-to-day HR operations reliably
3. Stay compliant with labor laws
4. Manage payroll, benefits, and HR finances
5. Handle employee relations and conflicts
6. Develop and retain talent
7. Manage performance and compensation
8. Plan workforce needs
9. Maintain company culture
10. Handle offboarding and exits
11. **Decide which roles need humans vs. AI agents**
12. **Get a full HR function without building a full HR team**

### Customer Pains
- HR is too expensive to staff properly
- Can't find good HR talent (talent shortage)
- Compliance is complex and risky
- HR quality is inconsistent person-to-person
- Too many disconnected tools
- No strategic HR thinking — just admin
- Slow response times to employees
- High turnover due to poor people practices
- No framework to decide which roles could be AI agents

### Customer Gains (What we deliver)
- **90% cost reduction** vs. a traditional HR department
- **Complete HR function** — all 5 divisions, not just a chatbot or a single tool
- **Instant deployment** — virtual employees are live in hours, not months
- **24/7 availability** — agents never sleep, take PTO, or quit
- **Always compliant** — agents auto-update with regulation changes
- **Consistent quality** — every employee gets the same excellent experience
- **Agent Factory** — submit any JD, find out if a virtual employee can do the job
- **Data-driven decisions** — workforce analytics and financial intelligence built in
- **Scalable** — from 10 to 10,000 employees, same subscription
- **One platform** — replaces 5–10 fragmented HR tools AND multiple HR headcount
