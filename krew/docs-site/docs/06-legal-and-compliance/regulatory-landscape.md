# HR Regulatory Landscape by Region

## Why This Matters
Our Compliance Agent must be accurate about labor laws. Getting this wrong exposes customers to lawsuits, fines, and reputational damage. This is both our biggest risk and our biggest competitive advantage.

## Priority Markets (Research & Build First)

### 1. United States
- Federal: FLSA, FMLA, ADA, Title VII, OSHA, COBRA, ERISA
- State-by-state employment laws (50 states, all different)
- At-will employment (most states)
- Key complexities: overtime rules, worker classification, leave laws
- [ ] Map state-by-state requirements for top 10 states

### 2. Saudi Arabia
- Saudi Labor Law (Royal Decree No. M/51)
- Saudization (Nitaqat) requirements
- GOSI (social insurance)
- End-of-service benefits (gratuity)
- Working hours and leave entitlements
- WPS (Wage Protection System)
- [ ] Deep-dive Saudi labor law documentation

### 3. UAE
- Federal Labour Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 33/2021)
- Free zone vs. mainland employment rules
- End-of-service gratuity
- WPS compliance
- Emiratisation requirements
- [ ] Map UAE federal + free zone requirements

### 4. United Kingdom
- Employment Rights Act 1996
- Working Time Regulations
- Equality Act 2010
- TUPE regulations
- Statutory sick pay, maternity/paternity
- [ ] UK employment law reference guide
