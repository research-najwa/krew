# AI in Employment — Legal Risks & Considerations

## Key Legal Risks

### 1. AI Bias in Hiring
- AI screening tools can discriminate based on protected characteristics
- NYC Local Law 144 requires bias audits for AI hiring tools
- EEOC has issued guidance on AI and employment discrimination
- **Mitigation:** Regular bias audits, diverse training data, human review of decisions

### 2. Automated Decision-Making
- GDPR Article 22: Right not to be subject to solely automated decisions
- EU AI Act classifies HR AI as "high risk" — requires conformity assessment
- **Mitigation:** Human-in-the-loop for all significant decisions (hiring, firing, promotions)

### 3. Liability for AI Advice
- If our Compliance Agent gives wrong advice, who is liable?
- Need clear terms of service disclaimers
- Professional liability insurance (E&O)
- **Mitigation:** Disclaimer that AI advice doesn't constitute legal advice; recommend attorney review

### 4. Employee Monitoring / Privacy
- Recording and analyzing employee communications raises privacy concerns
- Must be transparent about what data is collected and how it's used
- **Mitigation:** Clear privacy notices, data minimization

### 5. Worker Classification
- Using AI to manage workers may affect employment relationship characterization
- **Mitigation:** Clear that we are a tool provider, not an employer
