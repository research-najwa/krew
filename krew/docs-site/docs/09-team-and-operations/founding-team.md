# Founding Team

## Ideal Founding Team Composition
A company at this intersection of HR + AI needs a team that covers three core areas:

### 1. CEO / Business Lead
- Deep understanding of HR industry and buyer personas
- Strong network in target market (HR leaders, CEOs, investors)
- Fundraising and storytelling ability
- Go-to-market and sales instinct
- **Ideal background:** HR executive, HR tech founder, management consulting

### 2. CTO / Technical Lead
- AI/ML engineering expertise (LLMs, agents, RAG)
- Full-stack engineering capability
- System architecture and scalability experience
- Security-conscious engineering mindset
- **Ideal background:** AI/ML engineer, startup CTO, AI product engineer

### 3. CPO / Product Lead (Can be combined with CEO or CTO initially)
- Product management experience in B2B SaaS
- UX/design sensibility for complex workflows
- Understanding of HR workflows and processes
- Data-driven decision making
- **Ideal background:** Product manager in HR tech, B2B SaaS PM

## Equity Split Considerations
- [ ] Equal split vs. contribution-based split
- [ ] Vesting schedule (standard: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff)
- [ ] Founder roles and decision rights
- [ ] What happens if a founder leaves? (Vesting protects against this)
- [ ] Advisor equity pool (typically 1–5%)
- [ ] Employee option pool (typically 10–15% at formation)

## Co-Founder Agreement Topics
- [ ] Equity split and vesting
- [ ] Roles and responsibilities
- [ ] Decision-making process (who has final say on what?)
- [ ] Time commitment (full-time requirement?)
- [ ] IP assignment
- [ ] Non-compete terms
- [ ] Separation terms (what if someone leaves?)
