# Financial Model — Instructions

## What to Build
Create a detailed financial model in Google Sheets or Excel with the following tabs:

### Tab 1: Assumptions
- Pricing by tier
- Customer acquisition rate by month
- Churn rate
- Average employees per customer
- Net revenue retention
- Team hiring plan with salaries
- Infrastructure cost per customer
- LLM cost per query / per customer
- Marketing spend by channel

### Tab 2: Revenue Model
- Monthly customer count (new, churned, net)
- Revenue by tier
- Total MRR and ARR
- Revenue growth rate
- ARPU trends

### Tab 3: Cost Model
- Team costs (salaries + benefits + equity)
- Infrastructure costs (cloud, LLM, third-party)
- Marketing and sales costs
- G&A (legal, accounting, office, insurance)
- Total monthly burn

### Tab 4: P&L (Profit & Loss)
- Revenue
- COGS
- Gross profit and margin
- Operating expenses (by category)
- EBITDA
- Net income/loss

### Tab 5: Cash Flow
- Starting cash
- Cash from operations
- Cash from financing (fundraising)
- Monthly cash balance
- Runway remaining

### Tab 6: Scenarios
- Bull case (fast growth, low churn)
- Base case (planned growth)
- Bear case (slow growth, high churn)

## Tools to Use
- Google Sheets (collaborative, free)
- Or Excel / Notion
- Consider: Causal.app, Runway Financial, or Pry for startup-specific modeling
