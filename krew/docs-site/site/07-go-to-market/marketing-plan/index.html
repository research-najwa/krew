    Day Content Type Channel
    Monday LinkedIn post (founder insight) LinkedIn

    • Blog posts (1,500–2,500 words, SEO-focused)
    • LinkedIn posts (personal brand of founders)
    • Case studies (customer success stories)
    • Webinars (monthly)
    • Whitepapers / guides (quarterly)
    • Short videos (product demos, explainers)
    • Podcast appearances (guest on HR and AI podcasts)
